# Welcome to Project Lighthouse

Project Lighthouse tests how frontier models handle real personal-finance questions.

Each task assigns you a **persona** (their background, goals, and a folder of 10 to 20 pre-loaded synthetic financial documents) and a **category**, like budgeting or tax planning. You write a complex prompt in that persona's voice, then grade three model rollouts against a rubric you author.

Prompt
→
Critical Elements
→
Failure Justifications
→
Golden Trajectory
→
Rubric

## No-AI rule

This is the one rule that gets you offboarded

Do not use any LLM, AI assistant, or AI generation tool to author any part of a task. This includes ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, character.ai, or anything else. The prompt, the failure justifications, and the rubric must all be your own writing.

**What you can and cannot use AI for**

| Topic | Good | Neutral | Bad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lookups | Use AI to look up a tax rule, a formula, or a definition you then re-verify. | Use AI to remind you of a calculation method you already know. | Use AI to compute the answer for your task. |

**Why the rule exists**

The value of Project Lighthouse is human judgment about what real people ask and what a good answer looks like. If you launder the work through an AI, you produce data that trains the next model on the previous model's mistakes. That is the failure mode the project exists to avoid.

# What you produce on every task

Every task ships 5 artifacts. All 5 are yours, end to end. The workspace is already built - you do not generate input files.

  

1. **Prompt** - 1 complex, layered question in the persona's voice, grounded in what you saw in the environment.
2. **Critical Elements** - the specific facts, constraints, and outputs a correct answer must hit.
3. **Failure Justifications** - for each of the 3 model rollouts that failed, a 3-5 sentence justification with quoted output.
4. **Golden Trajectory** - the ideal response you had in mind when you wrote the prompt.
5. **Rubric Edits** - 20 to 35 weighted criteria, scored PASS / FAIL against each model, with difficulty and discrimination bars confirmed.

When you claim a task, the platform shows you 2 pieces of context. You author the prompt **FOR** this persona, **ABOUT** this category. The combination shapes everything: the question you ask, the answer you have in mind, and every criterion in the rubric.

## Persona

The persona is your raw material

On each task, you'll see a persona for one of our retail consumers. Their bank statement, transaction history, and any other workspace files are already loaded in the sandbox, so read them before you write a single word of the prompt. Let the persona's life, occupation, income, family, where they shop, what they owe, leak into the prompt you write.

  

A prompt that could have been written by anyone is too generic. Pull at least two specific details from the workspace into the question - names, dollar amounts, employer, dependents, debts. That specificity is what makes the model do real work.

  

Full instructions [here](https://project-lighthouse-instructions.learn.joinhandshake.com/personas).

## Category

One of 6 in-scope topics. Your prompt must clearly land in the assigned category.

In scope

* Budgeting / spending analysis
* Subscriptions / cash flow
* Retail investment + portfolio analysis
* Credit decisions
* Goal-based / scenario planning

Out of scope

* Tax planning (1040-level individual)
* IB or corporate finance
* Institutional asset management
* Specific securities recommendations (regulated advice)

## Time expectations

* **15 to 20 minutes** reviewing the environment
* **30 minutes** writing the prompt
* *Model rollouts run while you wait*
* **15 to 20 minutes** reviewing the model responses
* **30 to 45 minutes** writing failure justifications
* **60 to 90 minutes** authoring the rubric and scoring

**Total: 2.5 to 3.5 hours per task.**

Two W-2s, a small brokerage account Renata opened, and a family HSA Curtis funds pre-tax through payroll plus $4,000 of their own money this year.
